Entries and exits

Sanitized position summaries, including each position’s observed maximum and minimum unrealized P&L over its holding period; option legs and raw quotes are excluded. Modeled expected return is expected net P&L divided by total reserve; full-credit probability uses the saved blended reference, or a Black–Scholes fallback marked (BS), reconstructed from saved entry delta/IV when needed. A dash means neither estimate was recorded. Q−P is the saved entry-time risk-neutral minus model probability of finishing in the loss region, in percentage points. Positive values mean option prices assign more loss risk than the entry model. Historical rows retain their original model, which may predate the blended reference.
